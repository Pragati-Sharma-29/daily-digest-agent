## Open Source & Developer Tools

### What happened
Tensions between Google's Play Store and open-source Android ecosystems surfaced today, with real consequences for privacy-conscious users. Separately, the developer community continued pushing the boundaries of local model inference on consumer hardware.

### Key stories
- **Google Play Store blocks AuroraStore, hurting GrapheneOS users** — Google's Play Store has blocked AuroraStore, an open-source client that allows users of privacy-focused Android distributions like GrapheneOS to access Play apps without a Google account. This is a significant blow to the de-Googled Android ecosystem and raises legitimate questions about whether platform operators should be able to restrict access to third-party app clients. ([Hacker News / GitLab](https://gitlab.com/AuroraOSS/AuroraStore/-/work_items/1566))
- **Google Play no longer allowing AnkiDroid to link to Open Collective for donations** — Google has blocked AnkiDroid, the popular open-source flashcard app, from linking to its Open Collective donation page. This is part of a broader pattern of Play Store policies tightening in ways that disadvantage open-source projects that don't use Google's own in-app billing. ([Hacker News / GitHub](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android/issues/21656))
- **Running 104GB Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on 48GB Mac at ~12 tokens/second** — A developer released SlotStream, a technique enabling a 104GB model to run on 48GB of consumer GPU memory at usable inference speeds. As frontier model sizes grow, techniques that make them accessible on prosumer hardware have outsized importance for privacy, cost, and local deployment use cases. ([Hacker News](https://github.com/carloslfu/slotstream))

### What to watch
The Play Store restrictions on AuroraStore and AnkiDroid are likely to generate regulatory attention in the EU, where the DMA is specifically designed to prevent this type of platform self-preferencing. Watch for formal complaints and whether Google modifies its policies under regulatory pressure.
