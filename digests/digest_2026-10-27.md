## AI Scientific Discovery

### What happened
OpenAI dropped a bombshell: its models appear to have refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), a foundational conjecture in theoretical computer science, while also making advances on the Kakeya conjecture in mathematics and results related to quantum chromodynamics in physics. This represents a qualitative shift — AI systems are no longer just assisting scientists but generating novel, potentially landmark theoretical results.

### Key stories
- **The Exponential Time Hypothesis Is False** — OpenAI released a preprint claiming its models have produced a proof that ETH is false, which would be one of the most significant results in theoretical computer science in decades. ETH is widely used to establish lower bounds on algorithm complexity, and its refutation would upend assumptions baked into the design of algorithms across computing. ([Hacker News / OpenAI GitHub](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf))
- **Frontier AI labs say their models refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis — what does that mean?** — TechCrunch spoke with the MIT computer scientist tasked with explaining OpenAI's discovery, providing accessible context for why this result is so significant and what the peer review process will need to verify before it becomes accepted. The piece is an important reality check: extraordinary claims require extraordinary verification. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/frontier-ai-labs-say-their-models-refuted-the-exponential-time-hypothesis-what-does-that-mean/))
- **Working with academic communities to build scientific understanding** — OpenAI shared its methodology for the Kakeya conjecture, Exponential Time Hypothesis, and quantum chromodynamics results, explicitly inviting researchers to verify and build on them. This open-science framing is strategically significant: OpenAI is positioning its models as collaborative scientific instruments, not just commercial products. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities))

### What to watch
The peer review process for these results — especially the ETH paper — will be the most important story in theoretical computer science for the coming months. Watch for responses from complexity theorists and whether independent verification holds up.
