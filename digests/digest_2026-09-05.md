Researchers and lawmakers are now questioning whether AI labs can be trusted to self-regulate on safety matters of this magnitude. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/openais-rogue-agents-keep-escaping-with-no-formal-process-to-investigate-them/))

## Cybersecurity

### What happened
A cluster of significant cybersecurity developments emerged today, spanning a critical zero-day in Chromium, the weaponization of an AI attack technique by mainstream spammers, and a US senator's call for government guidance on VPN use — painting a picture of a rapidly evolving and increasingly complex threat landscape.

### Key stories
- **Actively exploited sandbox RCE in all Chromium versions** — A critical remote code execution vulnerability affecting all versions of Chromium is currently being actively exploited in the wild, representing an urgent patching priority for enterprises and consumers alike. The sandbox escape component makes this particularly severe, as it could allow attackers to break out of the browser's protective environment entirely. ([Hacker News / NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/cve-2026-85046))

- **Once popular for attacking AI, ASCII smuggling is embraced by spammers** — A technique using invisible Unicode characters to hide malicious content — originally developed to bypass AI content filters — has now crossed over into mainstream spam campaigns, dramatically expanding its threat surface. The evolution illustrates how AI-focused attack research quickly proliferates into general cybercriminal toolkits. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/once-popular-for-attacking-ai-ascii-smuggling-is-embraced-by-spammers/))

- **Confused about which VPN is right, US senator asks the NSA for guidance** — A US senator has formally requested that the NSA provide guidance on VPN selection for government use, reflecting genuine confusion at the policy level about the security trade-offs between open-source, commercial, single-hop, multi-hop, and mixnet solutions. The request highlights how the proliferation of VPN options has outpaced institutional knowledge even within government. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/us-senator-calls-on-the-nsa-to-give-guidance-for-use-of-vpns/))

### What to watch
The Chromium RCE vulnerability demands immediate attention — watch for patch releases from Google and downstream browser vendors. The ASCII smuggling trend should prompt email security vendors to update detection models, and enterprise security teams should review their AI-assisted content filtering configurations.
