The framing positions AlloyDB as the definitive answer to agentic database architecture — a strong claim, but one backed by meaningful engineering. ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/databases/alloydbs-agentic-database-architecture/))

([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/containers-kubernetes/gke-agentic-migration/))

## Security: RSA Vulnerability & AI-Assisted Attacks

### What happened
Today brought two major security stories that together paint a troubling picture: foundational cryptographic assumptions are being challenged at the algorithmic level, while AI is simultaneously lowering the barrier for mass-scale credential compromise attacks.

### Key stories
- **There's a new way to break RSA that's faster than anything we've seen before** — Ars Technica reports that researchers have discovered a new RSA-breaking technique that doesn't rely solely on integer factoring — previously considered the essential hard problem underlying RSA's security. The new approach is faster than any previously known method, which, depending on the specifics, could have significant implications for the long-term viability of RSA in high-security applications. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/))
- **Microsoft disrupts AI-assisted platform that compromised 12,000 accounts** — Microsoft announced it has disrupted EvilTokens, an end-to-end platform that uses AI to automate mass account compromises, which had already breached 12,000 accounts. EvilTokens represents a new class of cybercriminal tooling where AI is used to scale, personalize, and accelerate attacks — what once required skilled operators can now be nearly automated. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/microsoft-disrupts-ai-assisted-platform-that-compromised-12000/))
- **Two-tier encryption in the UK** — A piece circulating on Hacker News examines the UK's push toward two-tier encryption frameworks, which would require providers to maintain backdoor access for law enforcement while offering standard encryption to ordinary users. This ongoing policy debate has major implications for global encryption standards, particularly as more personal AI systems store sensitive user data. ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/))

### What to watch
The RSA story needs independent verification and peer review — watch for the cryptography research community's response over the coming days. On the AI-assisted attack side, expect Microsoft and the broader security industry to publish more detail about EvilTokens' techniques, which will likely influence defensive tooling priorities.

([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/govt-starts-consultation-to-develop-indigenous-chips-modules-for-power-grids/articleshow/134462884.cms))

### What to watch
