([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994064/anthropic-spent-this-week-in-hot-water-over-cybersecurity))

- **ChatGPT fabricates police testimony in murder appeal** — The New Mexico Supreme Court fined attorney Stephen Aarons and held him in contempt after he submitted a court filing containing ChatGPT-hallucinated fake police testimony in a murder appeal — testimony he failed to verify. This case is among the most serious AI hallucination consequences to reach a state supreme court and will likely accelerate mandatory verification requirements for AI-assisted legal filings. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/chatgpt-invented-fake-police-testimony-in-murder-appeal-new-mexico-high-court-says/articleshow/134080805.cms))

### What to watch

## Cybersecurity Threats

### What happened
Two major attack vectors dominated security news today: ClickFix social engineering attacks spreading virally across platforms, and a shared exploit kit being leveraged by four distinct threat groups against Chrome and Windows. The Trezor email provider breach added a crypto dimension to the threat landscape.

### Key stories
- **ClickFix attacks go viral on PCs and Macs** — ClickFix is a social engineering technique that exploits user frustration by presenting fake error messages and urging users to run malicious commands to "fix" them. Its virality stems from its simplicity and the psychological exploitation of users already trying to get things done — making it effective across both Windows and macOS. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/clickfix-attacks-infecting-pcs-and-macs-are-going-viral/))

- **Four threat groups share the same Chrome and Windows exploit kit** — Security researchers identified four distinct threat actors using an identical exploit kit targeting Chrome and Windows vulnerabilities. AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery is cited as a likely contributor to the shortened gap between vulnerability identification and exploitation in the wild. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/09/4-groups-caught-using-the-same-chrome-and-windows-exploit-kit/))

- **Trezor email provider breach exposes hundreds of thousands of crypto owners** — Hardware wallet maker Trezor confirmed a second breach of its email provider, with scammers already targeting the exposed user base. That this is Trezor's second such vendor breach makes this a serious supply chain / third-party risk management failure. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/scammers-target-hundreds-of-thousands-of-crypto-owners-after-trezor-confirms-data-breach-of-email-provider/))

- **Texas judge rules TikTok misled users on child safety** — A Texas judge ruled that TikTok deceived users by claiming it would remove content violating Community Guidelines while internally classifying some such material as merely "hard to find" rather than removing it. This ruling has significant implications for platform trust, child safety regulation, and TikTok's ongoing regulatory battles in the US. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/texas-judge-rules-tiktok-misled-users-on-child-safety-feature/articleshow/134081284.cms))

### What to watch
The shared exploit kit discovery is particularly alarming — watch for Google and Microsoft emergency patches. The ClickFix spread warrants monitoring for whether it evolves into ransomware deployment at scale.

### What happened

### Key stories

- **MIT TR 35 Under 35 highlights biotech's next generation** — MIT Technology Review's annual 35 Innovators Under 35 list focuses this year on biotech, showcasing researchers working across gene therapy, synthetic biology, AI-driven drug discovery, and diagnostics. ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/11/1143834/meet-the-under-35s-shaping-the-future-of-biotech/))

- **Google's ToolGrad enables efficient tool-use dataset generation** — Google Research published work on ToolGrad, a method using textual "gradients" to efficiently generate datasets for training AI models to use external tools. ([Google Research](https://research.google/blog/toolgrad-efficient-tool-use-dataset-generation-with-textual-gradients/))

### What to watch
The antimicrobial resistance application of AI is one of the most consequential potential use cases — watch for clinical validation of computationally discovered compounds. Google's ToolGrad methodology, if adopted broadly, could meaningfully accelerate agentic AI capability development.
