## General AI Trends & Philosophical Discussions

### What happened
Beyond specific applications and models, the broader discourse around AI delves into its fundamental nature, potential trajectory, and societal implications. Experts are challenging linear thinking about AI development, discussing its impact on job markets, and exploring frameworks to measure progress towards AGI.

### Key stories
- **Mustafa Suleyman: AI development won’t hit a wall anytime soon—here’s why** — Mustafa Suleyman argues that humanity's linear thinking often fails to grasp the exponential nature of AI development, suggesting that progress will continue unimpeded for the foreseeable future due to compounding innovation cycles. ([MIT_Tech_Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/08/1135398/mustafa-suleyman-ai-future/))
- **Measuring progress toward AGI: A cognitive framework** — Google DeepMind is introducing a cognitive framework to better measure progress towards Artificial General Intelligence and has launched a Kaggle hackathon to develop relevant evaluations, highlighting the need for standardized and robust metrics. ([Google_DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/measuring-progress-toward-agi-a-cognitive-framework/))
- **The Download: AI’s impact on jobs, and data centres in space** — This digest touches upon the significant impact of AI on job markets, emphasizing the need for robust data to truly understand the shifts, alongside the emerging trend of deploying data centers in space. ([MIT_Tech_Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/07/1135208/the-download-ai-impact-jobs-data-centres-space/))
- **From Hierarchy to Intelligence** — Sequoia Capital discusses a fundamental shift from hierarchical organizational structures to intelligence-driven systems, implying that AI will transform not just individual tasks but the very fabric of how organizations operate. ([Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence/))
- **A conversation with Kevin Scott: What’s next in AI** — Microsoft's Kevin Scott shares insights into the future of AI, touching upon upcoming trends and breakthroughs, emphasizing the continuous evolution and integration of AI into diverse aspects of technology. ([Microsoft_AI](https://blogs.microsoft.com/ai/a-conversation-with-kevin-scott-whats-next-in-ai/))

### What to watch
Observe the evolution of AI's societal narrative, particularly concerning job displacement and wealth distribution, as well as advancements in the theoretical understanding and measurable benchmarks for AGI.
