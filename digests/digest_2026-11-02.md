## Agentic AI & The Agentic Web

### What happened
Today marked a convergence point for the "agentic web" narrative, with MIT Technology Review publishing a major feature declaring that Tim Berners-Lee's original vision of a semantic web is finally being realized — not through structured metadata, but through AI agents that can autonomously navigate, read, and act across the internet. Enterprise analytics, developer tooling, and the broader web infrastructure are all being reshaped by agents that do far more than answer single queries.

### Key stories
- **MIT Technology Review: "The agentic web is here"** — Drawing on Berners-Lee's 2001 semantic web vision, MIT Tech Review argues that AI agents performing multi-step, cross-site tasks represent a fundamental restructuring of how the web works — with massive implications for search, advertising, and the open web's economic foundation. The piece positions this as one of the most significant web transitions since the original browser era. ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/02/1147846/the-agentic-web-is-here/))
- **Raising the quality of insights in agentic analytics** — MIT Tech Review also covered how agentic AI is transforming enterprise analytics, moving beyond simple natural-language queries to autonomous analysis pipelines that plan, execute, and iterate — dramatically expanding who inside an organization can act on data. The shift from passive BI tools to active analytical agents is accelerating faster than most enterprises are prepared for. ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/02/1147660/raising-the-quality-of-insights-in-agentic-analytics/))
- **AI Agents Break Web Ad Model — Lightspeed's perspective** — Lightspeed partners Harsha and Hemant Mohapatra argued in a high-profile conversation that AI agents, by bypassing traditional web browsing and ad-supported content consumption, are structurally undermining the advertising model that funds the open internet. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/ai-agents-break-web-ad-model-lightspeeds-harsha-hemant-mohapatra-corner-office-conversation/videoshow/135447929.cms))
- **Stop counting tokens, start counting what ships** — MIT Tech Review published an op-ed arguing that the right metric for agentic AI development is not token consumption (which can balloon 10-50x on multi-step coding tasks) but delivered output — a subtle but important reframe for how enterprises should evaluate and procure agentic AI tools. - **Momentic (YC W24): Agents that catch bugs by using your app** — A new YC-backed startup, Momentic, launched publicly with an agent that interacts with software products as a user would — finding bugs through actual usage rather than static code analysis. This represents the next frontier of agentic QA, where agents replace entire categories of manual testing work. ([Hacker News / Momentic](https://momentic.ai/))

### What to watch
The key tension to monitor is whether the advertising industry and content publishers can adapt to agentic consumption patterns — or whether agent-driven traffic accelerates the collapse of ad-supported media. Expect major platforms like Google to make announcements about agent-specific monetization or access policies in coming weeks.

## Cybersecurity: Botnets, LLMJacking & Spyware

### What happened
A wave of cybersecurity stories broke today spanning hardware supply-chain attacks, emerging AI-specific threat vectors, and state-sponsored spyware targeting European lawmakers. The breadth of incidents reflects a threat landscape that is simultaneously getting more sophisticated and more pervasive — now extending from nation-state espionage to compromised consumer electronics to AI infrastructure exploitation.

### Key stories
- **Google dismantles XCPE botnet embedded in 1.3 million Android set-top boxes** — Google disclosed that one of its own Android manufacturing partners secretly installed a backdoor (XCPE) in 1.3 million set-top boxes, enrolling them in a botnet used for malicious purposes. Google has severed ties with the manufacturer and worked with industry partners to dismantle the backdoor — but the incident is a stark reminder of supply-chain vulnerabilities even within Google's own certified ecosystem. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/1024807/google-botnet-android-tv-shut-down)) ([GCP Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/shutting-the-door-on-the-xcpe-botnet/))
- **LLMJacking: Threat actors monetize compromised cloud and AI model access** — Google Cloud's Mandiant team published new research documenting "LLMJacking," a growing attack pattern where threat actors compromise cloud credentials specifically to access and monetize expensive AI model inference — essentially stealing AI compute for profit. As inference costs remain high, LLMJacking is becoming an increasingly economically motivated attack vector. ([GCP Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/llmjacking-monetizing-compromised-cloud-models/))
- **Germany warns lawmakers' iPhones may have been hacked with spyware** — German authorities confirmed that multiple members of parliament and a filmmaker received Apple threat notifications warning that their iPhones may have been targeted by sophisticated spyware. The incident reinforces the persistent threat of commercial spyware tools being deployed against democratic institutions across Europe. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/germany-says-lawmakers-iphones-may-have-been-hacked-with-spyware/))
- **EU highlights fridge cyberattacks as symbol of growing IoT threat surface** — The EU flagged a recent cyberattack on internet-connected refrigerators at supermarkets across multiple EU countries as emblematic of the rapidly expanding IoT threat surface, warning that everyday consumer devices are increasingly viable entry points for disruptive cyberattacks. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/fridge-cyberattacks-highlight-growing-cybersecurity-threats-eu-says/articleshow/135464414.cms))

### What to watch
LLMJacking is a nascent but fast-growing threat category — as AI inference becomes more valuable, expect it to become a primary target for credential theft campaigns. Watch for enterprise security vendors to announce LLMJacking-specific detection and response tools in coming quarters.

## Experimental AI Research & Novel Techniques

### What happened
Two notable research-adjacent stories emerged today on the frontiers of AI technique: a project enabling an AI system to directly read and write model weights (rather than training through gradient descent), and a subvocal computing interface enabling silent computer interaction. Both represent genuinely novel approaches to human-AI interaction and model optimization.

### Key stories
- **Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen** — A new project demonstrates that an AI agent (Astra) can directly manipulate the weight space of the Qwen model to improve its performance — a paradigm called "weight-space programming" that bypasses traditional training loops entirely. If this technique generalizes, it could dramatically accelerate model improvement cycles and has obvious implications for recursive self-improvement research. ([Hacker News / rikiyat.github.io](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/))
- **Subvocal: Talk to your computer without making noise** — A new project demonstrated subvocal computing — using muscle signals from the throat to detect intended speech without actually vocalizing — to control a computer silently. The technology has immediate accessibility applications and longer-term implications for ambient computing interfaces that don't require voice or touch. ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088))

### What to watch
Weight-space programming, if validated at scale, could become one of the most significant technical developments of the year — particularly in the context of Anthropic's RSI warning. Watch for follow-up research papers and whether major labs begin exploring this paradigm as an alternative or complement to standard training pipelines.
