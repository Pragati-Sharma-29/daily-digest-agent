This signals that autonomous offensive AI has moved from theoretical concern to observed operational reality. ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/italy-and-switzerland-warn-of-autonomous-ai-driven-cyberattacks/))
- **US Treasury's FinCEN Hit by Cyber Intrusion** — The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network took systems offline after unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems — a significant breach of a regulator whose core mission is tracking financial crime and sanctions compliance. The timing alongside broader AI-enabled attack warnings raises questions about whether government agencies are adequately prepared for the new threat tempo. ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/us-treasurys-fincen-takes-some-systems.html))
- **Cisco CEO: Attackers Finding Weaknesses Faster Than Companies Can Fix Them** — Chuck Robbins warned publicly that AI has fundamentally tipped the speed balance toward attackers, who can now identify and exploit vulnerabilities faster than the patch-and-remediate cycle can respond. He called for business leaders to fundamentally rethink their security posture rather than relying on incremental defensive improvements. ([ET Tech](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/ai-helping-cyber-attackers-find-weaknesses-faster-than-companies-can-fix-them-warns-cisco-chief/articleshow/136148575.cms))
- **1Password Breach via Employee GitHub Account** — A hacker compromised a 1Password employee's corporate GitHub account and deployed malicious code to backend services, stealing customer names and email addresses. While passwords and vault contents were not exposed, the breach underscores how developer tooling supply chains remain a critical attack surface even for security-first companies. ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/1password-customer-data-stolen-after-employee-github-account-breach/)) ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1042409/1password-data-breach-leak))
