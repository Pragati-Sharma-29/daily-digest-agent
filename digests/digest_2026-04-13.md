## AI in Business Operations

### What happened
AI is rapidly infiltrating core business functions beyond coding, with CFO suites adopting AI for financial experiments and operations teams leveraging tools like ChatGPT to streamline workflows. This shift represents AI moving from a niche technical tool to a pervasive operational asset, transforming everything from content handling to contact centers and influencing investment strategies towards "services as software."

### Key stories
*   **First coding, next finance? AI adoption comes to the CFO suite** — While AI has notably transformed coding, it is now quietly making significant inroads into finance, with CFOs initiating their own AI experiments to enhance financial processes. This indicates a broader, enterprise-wide adoption of AI, moving beyond traditional tech departments to impact critical strategic and operational functions. ([Battery_Ventures](https://www.battery.com/blog/first-coding-next-finance-ai-adoption-comes-to-the-cfo-suite/))
*   **How AI is replacing the analytical hierarchy in enterprise software** — AI is fundamentally changing enterprise software by replacing the traditional analytical hierarchy, rather than merely accelerating it, as argued by Jack Dorsey and Roelof Botha. This paradigm shift suggests AI will enable more autonomous decision-making and data processing within organizations, decentralizing traditional power structures. ([Insight_Partners](https://www.insightpartners.com/ideas/ai-is-replacing-the-analytical-hierarchy-in-enterprise-software/))
*   **Services: The New Software** — This article posits that "services" are emerging as the new form of "software," particularly within the enterprise context, challenging the traditional SaaS playbook that often offloaded implementation. This signals a shift where integrated service delivery, potentially powered by AI, becomes a core offering rather than an outsourced component, emphasizing a more holistic approach to enterprise solutions. ([Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software/))
*   **ChatGPT for operations teams** — OpenAI provides guidance on how operations teams can leverage ChatGPT to streamline workflows, enhance coordination, standardize processes, and accelerate execution. This resource demonstrates the practical applications of large language models in improving day-to-day business operations across various industries. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/academy/operations))

### What to watch
Expect to see more specialized AI applications tailored for specific business functions, particularly in finance, HR, and customer service. The trend of "services as software" will likely continue, pushing companies to integrate AI-powered service delivery more deeply into their core product offerings, and the role of AI in organizational decision-making will grow.

## Top Story

The burgeoning world of AI agents is rapidly reshaping the enterprise technology landscape, moving from theoretical concepts to practical, high-impact applications. This fundamental shift is underscored by "Agent Skills Are the New SDK" from Battery Ventures, which argues that AI agents are becoming the primary mechanism for software distribution and integration within developer infrastructure. This means that instead of traditional SDKs, developers will increasingly build modular "skills" that enable AI agents to perform specialized tasks, seamlessly integrating new functionalities into existing agentic workflows. Companies like Zapier already demonstrate this by deploying more AI agents than human employees for internal operations, while giants like Google, OpenAI, and Salesforce are embedding agentic capabilities into their core products, from enterprise cloud solutions to workplace assistants. This transition signifies AI's evolution beyond mere automation to autonomous decision-making and complex task execution, fundamentally altering how businesses will build, operate, and scale in the coming years. ([Battery_Ventures](https://www.battery.com/blog/agent-skills-are-the-new-sdk-and-you-should-be-building-one/))
