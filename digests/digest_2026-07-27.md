## Semiconductors & Memory Technology

### What happened
Two significant semiconductor stories broke today. China is reportedly delivering its first domestically produced DUV lithography tools to its top chipmakers this year — a major milestone in its push for self-sufficiency. Meanwhile, Asianometry published a deep technical analysis of True 3D DRAM, explaining why vertical stacking is the only remaining path to significant memory density gains.

### Key stories
- **China's homegrown DUV chipmaking tools arrive at SMIC and others** — According to reporting sourced from The Information, Chinese-made DUV tools are being delivered to SMIC, Hua Hong Semiconductor, and ChangXin Memory Technologies in 2026, representing a significant step toward reducing dependence on ASML and other Western equipment suppliers. While DUV (as opposed to EUV) represents an older generation of lithography, achieving domestic capability is strategically important for China's semiconductor roadmap under ongoing export controls. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/china-begins-making-homegrown-duv-chipmaking-tools-the-information/articleshow/132664249.cms))

- **True 3D DRAM: the next memory scaling frontier** — Asianometry's analysis explains that DRAM has exhausted most lateral (2D) scaling paths, and that stacking memory cells vertically — analogous to what 3D NAND did for flash storage — is the primary remaining lever for density gains. This matters enormously for AI infrastructure, since memory bandwidth and capacity are key bottlenecks for large model inference and training. ([Asianometry](https://asianometry.passport.online/member/episode/true-3-d-dram))

### What to watch
Track whether China's DUV tools achieve yields comparable to ASML's mature DUV equipment — yield is the real test of whether domestic tools are production-ready or merely symbolic. For 3D DRAM, watch for announcements from Samsung, SK Hynix, or Micron on commercialization timelines.
