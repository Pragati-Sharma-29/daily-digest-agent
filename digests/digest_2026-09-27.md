([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/bill-gates-joins-calls-for-ai-safeguards-including-legislation/articleshow/134523669.cms))
- **Anthropic CEO Amodei to have White House dinner with Trump** — Dario Amodei's dinner with President Trump, reported by Axios, represents a significant political moment for Anthropic and the AI industry broadly. ## Cybersecurity

### What happened
Two major cybersecurity stories broke today: the ShinyHunters hacker group has significantly expanded its attack surface to include Oracle's PeopleSoft platform, and Google's advertising network was found delivering scareware to everyday users. Both incidents illustrate the dual threat landscape — sophisticated state-adjacent threat actors targeting enterprise infrastructure, and commodity scammers exploiting trusted consumer platforms.

### Key stories
- **ShinyHunters hackers expanded attacks on Oracle's PeopleSoft, Google says** — Google has disclosed that the ShinyHunters group, previously known for large-scale data breaches at consumer platforms, has expanded its operations to target Oracle's PeopleSoft enterprise HR and ERP system. The escalation is alarming because PeopleSoft holds sensitive employee data for major corporations and government agencies, and a successful breach could have cascading downstream consequences. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/shinyhunters-hackers-expanded-attacks-on-oracles-peoplesoft-google-says/articleshow/134522003.cms))
- **Google ads caught delivering convincing scareware to unsuspecting users** — Ars Technica reports that ads appearing across the internet are displaying fake security warnings designed to deceive users into calling fraudulent support lines or downloading malware. The fact that these ads are appearing through Google's network — one of the most scrutinized ad platforms globally — underscores the persistent difficulty of policing programmatic advertising at scale. ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/google-ads-caught-delivering-convincing-scareware-ads-to-unsuspecting-users/))

### What to watch
Oracle will need to issue a formal response to the PeopleSoft attack disclosures — watch for patch advisories and enterprise customer notifications. The Google ad scareware issue is perennial but worth monitoring for any policy response from Google or regulatory action from the FTC.

## Prediction Markets & Fintech Regulation

### What happened
A significant legal setback for prediction markets: a US appeals court ruled that states can regulate prediction market contracts, dealing a blow to Kalshi's argument that its sports event contracts are federally regulated "swaps" under the CFTC's exclusive jurisdiction.

### Key stories
- **US appeals court rules against Kalshi, says states can regulate prediction markets** — The 6th Circuit Court of Appeals found that Kalshi failed to demonstrate that its sports event contracts qualify as "swaps" subject to exclusive federal CFTC oversight, opening the door for a patchwork of state-level regulation. This ruling could significantly complicate Kalshi's national expansion strategy and sets a precedent that may chill investment in the broader prediction market sector. ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/us-appeals-court-rules-against-kalshi-says-states-can-regulate-prediction-markets/articleshow/134521955.cms))

### What to watch
Watch for Kalshi's response — whether they seek en banc review or escalate to the Supreme Court. The ruling's implications extend to all prediction market operators attempting to operate nationally under a single federal regulatory umbrella.
