([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/06/us-offers-10-million-for-info-on-group-behind-signal-and-whatsapp-hacking-spree/))
- **Lightspeed invests in Straiker** — Lightspeed Venture Partners announced an investment in Straiker, an AI security startup. ## Gaming & Digital Ownership

### What happened
Sony made two sweeping announcements today that together represent a fundamental shift away from physical and legacy digital gaming: ending disc production for new PlayStation games in 2028, and shutting down the PS3 and PS Vita digital stores. The moves sparked significant backlash from the gaming community around preservation concerns.

### Key stories
- **Sony ends physical PlayStation disc production in January 2028** — Sony announced that starting January 2028, it will no longer produce physical discs for new PlayStation game releases, completing the platform's shift to an all-digital model. The move is economically rational given that digital purchases now dominate, but it eliminates the secondary market, restricts consumer resale rights, and raises serious questions about long-term game access when digital storefronts inevitably close. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/01/sony-to-end-physical-playstation-game-discs-in-2028/))
- **Sony is killing discs — and showing us why it's a terrible idea** — The Verge argues compellingly that Sony's disc decision is a disaster for video game preservation, as physical media represents the most reliable long-term archive for games. Without discs, future historians and players will be entirely dependent on Sony's willingness to maintain servers and licensing agreements — a precarious foundation for cultural preservation. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/960212/sony-playstation-killing-discs-digital-preservation))
- **Sony is closing the PS3 and Vita digital stores** — Sony announced it will shut down PlayStation Store access on PS3 and PS Vita, closing off digital purchases for both legacy platforms. Combined with the disc production end, this creates a pattern where older Sony games systematically become inaccessible — a troubling trajectory for the industry's cultural heritage. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/960168/sony-playstation-ps3-vita-store-shut-down))

### What to watch
Watch for regulatory and legislative responses in Europe or the US around digital ownership rights and game preservation requirements — Sony's moves may accelerate advocacy efforts that have been building for years around the "right to repair and preserve" in gaming.
